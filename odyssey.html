Congratulations, you've made it to my corner of the internet. This page is dedicated to wildest show on television, The Odyssey.

The Odyssey is a Canadian TV show that aired from 1992 until 1995 about Jay, a boy who is transported to an alternate reality, Downworld, while in a coma from a bad fall. Accompanied by Flash and Alpha, Jay must navigate this complex new world to find his way home.


Downworld is a workd without adults. It is ruled by Brad, the oldest kid. Posters praising Brad's leadership can be seen on every street corner. Kids are part of different clubs often competing with each other. At first, everything that happens in real life seems to be mirrored in Downworld. It's presented as Jay's subconscious way to deal with his father (Brad) abandoning him years prior. The truth is more complicated. As Jay's journey progresses, it becomes clear than not all of it is subconscious.

Meet the characters:

  • Jay Ziegler: Jay is the main protagonist, a 12 year old boy who fell in a coma after falling from a tree house. Jay's main goal in Downworld is to find his father and get answers to what really happened.
  • Donna/Alpha: Jay's best friend in Upworld. In Downworld, she is Alpha, member of the book club.
  • Keith/Flash: Jay's nemesis in the pilot episode, Keith is partly responsible for Jay's fall from the tree house. Keith befriends Donna and help take care of Jay at Driftwood. In Downworld, Flash is a warrior who helps Jay find his father.

  • Medea/Sierra Jones: Paart of the Tower Counil, Medea is a powerful entity in Downworld. She labels Jay as the wrecker, the one person who could destroy her world by bring adults in it. Medea has a counterpart in Upworld, Sierra Jones, who is also in a coma at Driftwood.
  • Macro: Formerly a trusted advisor of Brad, Macro got rid of Brad and took his place to rule Downworld. Macro hunts Jay down for fear his will bring an end to their world.
  • Finger: Head monitor and Macro's second in command. Finger gradually gains more power and take Macro's place as supreme leader.
  • Brad: Jay's father, Brad, disappeared when Jay was 6. Initially presumed dead after a fishing boat accident, Brad resurfaces in Downworld.
  • Fractal: Also part of the Tower Council. Fractal is a scientist who investigates the true nature of Downworld, theorizing that there might be more beyond it.

Was it all in Jay's head?

While initially presented as Jay's subconscious way to deal with his father's disappearance, various clues suggest that Downwold is actually an alternate reality:

  • In the season one finale, it is revealed that Medea is also a coma patient in the same facility Jay is being cared for. She uses the vest of power to go back and forth between the two worlds. The season 2 premiere acknowledges this fact when Macro tells Medea she's been gone for weeks, to which Medea replies "I've been to Jay's world".

  • Furthermore, Medea always seem to know more than anyone else around her. She knows about the portal in the lighthouse, and even tells Jay she'd been here before. In season 2 episode 8, she tells Jay that a part of chidren who get abandoned come to downworld.
  • Jay's dad Brad, also seem to be the real deal in Downworld, and not a figment of Jay's imagination. In saeson 2 episode 9, Donna finds a picture of Medea and Brad. It's never fully explained but Medea says that Jay's dad warned her Jay was coming. In the season 2 finale, Brad is seen visiting Jay at Driftwood, trying to explain his whereabouts of the past 6 years in an attempt to give Jay closure, and then leaved. In the next scene, Jay wakes up his father in Downworld who says "I thought I left you up there!". This strongly suggests that Brad had been hiding in Downwold this whole time. He may have found the portal himself after the fishing boat accident brought him to the lighthouse.

  • Finally, in season 1, episode 12, Donna is seen taking a nap at Driftwood, at Jay's side where she has a dream of Medea. Again, this clearly means that Downworld is an alternate reality and not an invention of Jay's mind.
