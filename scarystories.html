The Old Woman

How far would you go to preserve your own identity? Jason is about to find out just how far he would go when an old woman becomes convinced he is the reincarnation of Jesus, sent back to Earth to warn humanity of impeding doom. Skeptical at first, Jason is forced to reconsider when a series of strange coincidences, followed by unexpected results from an ancestry DNA test complicate things. There's only one problem: Jason likes his life and wants nothing to do with religion.
