How far would you go to preserve your own identity? Jason is about to find out just how far he would go when an old woman suddenly claims he is not who he thinks he is. Jason was not a particular religious person growing up. Now in his early twenties, church was rarely on his mind. In fact, he woke up this morning to a phone call from his mother asking him if he will attend today's church service. Jason already had a lot on his plate, colege finals had taken up all of his free time. His final bilogy project consisting among other things in an ancestry DNA test was coming due next week. When he wasn't studying, he was working a full time job at a tech firm doing mostly entry level tasks. Still, Jason tried his best to make time for his family and reluctantly agreed to attench today's service.

What started as an ordinary church service took a turn Jason would have never expected. Sitting there listening to the sermon, Jason spots an old woman staring at him from a distance. He turns around to see if she might be looking at somebody else, but that was not the case. Intrigued, he started wondering what this was all about. He had never seen this lady before, she had white hair, was wearing all black and had a serious expression on her face. After the service, the old woman started walking towards Jason. Noticing the scene, Jason took a few steps back until he was cornered by the woman. She looks at him and says: "You are the reincarnation of Jesus. You must come with me."

Jason, in total disbelief, cannot utter a single word as he tries to process the odd sequence of events that just unfolded. His dad just a few feet away sees the distress on Jason's face. He walks over to his son to ask him if everything is alright before telling the old woman to get lost. They both look at each other perplexed by what had just happened as they watch the old woman walk away.

A few days go by, Jason had mostly forgotten about the incident and moved on with his busy life when he hears someone knocking at his door. His phone goes off, a package had just been delivered to his appartment. His ancestry DNA kit he had submitted two weeks ago was finally ready. He opens the app on his phone with excitement even though he already had a pretty good idea of his heritage. Afterall, both his mother and father were Irish, so he expected the results to be in line with what his parents told him. Unfortunately for Jason, the results would not be what he'd come to expect. There it was in black and white, 100% of Middle Eastern descent.

Confused, Jason rushes home to confront his mother. She breaks down. How could this be Jason asks. His mother explains that the timing of her pregnancy always felt off. She never knew who the father was, and in her mind, she was convinced that none of her previous partners could have gotten her pregnant. In fact, she had been single for six months when she found out she was pregnant.

Who is this woman?
